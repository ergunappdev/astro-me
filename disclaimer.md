# Disclaimer

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Soulastra is built for entertainment, curiosity and self-reflection. Please read this page so you know how to use the app's content wisely.

## 1. For entertainment and self-reflection

Astrology, tarot, numerology, Human Design, dream interpretation, palm reading and coffee reading are spiritual and cultural traditions. They are **not scientifically proven** methods of predicting the future or explaining personality. Every reading, forecast, score and suggestion in Soulastra is offered for entertainment and to support self-reflection. None of it is a statement of fact, a promise or a prediction that will come true.

## 2. Not professional advice

Nothing in the app is a substitute for advice from a qualified professional:

- **Health:** Our content is not medical, psychological or psychiatric advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor or mental health professional about your physical or mental health. Do not stop or change any treatment because of a reading.
- **Money:** Our content is not financial or investment advice. Do not make decisions about investments, purchases, debt or your career based on a reading.
- **Law:** Our content is not legal advice. Consult a lawyer about legal matters.
- **Relationships:** Readings and compatibility scores cannot tell you how a relationship will go or whether someone is right for you. Do not end or start a relationship, or make other major life decisions, based on them.

Your choices are your own. Please use your own judgment and seek professional help when needed.

## 3. AI-generated content

Luna's answers, personal readings, the yearly report and dream, palm and coffee readings are generated by artificial intelligence. AI can produce answers that are wrong, incomplete, inconsistent or that sound more certain than they are. Two answers to the same question may differ. AI output is not the opinion of an astrologer or any real person, and we do not check each answer individually.

## 4. Calculations and accuracy

Planet positions in Soulastra are calculated astronomically and are highly precise. Readings, however, depend on the information you provide: if your birth time is unknown or wrong, your rising sign, houses and some readings may not apply to you. Small differences between Soulastra and other apps or astrologers can come from different house systems, zodiac systems and interpretation traditions.

## 5. Palm and coffee reading

Palm and coffee readings are generated by AI from the photos you send and are symbolic interpretations. Photo quality, light and angle affect the result. These readings say nothing about your health, lifespan or future.

## 6. Soulmate and meeting people

- The compatibility score is a fun indicator based on astrological signs; it is not a measure of how well two people will actually get along.
- We do not verify users' identities, ages, backgrounds or statements. People are not always who they say they are.
- Never send money, gifts, financial details, ID documents or intimate images to people you meet in the app. Report anyone who asks for them.
- If you decide to meet someone, meet in a busy public place, tell someone you trust where you will be, arrange your own transport and stay in control of your drinks and belongings.
- If you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, leave and report the person. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

## 7. If you are struggling

Soulastra is not a crisis service. If you are thinking about harming yourself or someone else, or if you are in danger, contact your local emergency number right away (for example 112 in Europe and Turkey, 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK). You can find free, confidential helplines in your country at findahelpline.com.
