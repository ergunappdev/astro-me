# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

When you use Astro Me, you trust us with personal information such as your birth details. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

This policy is designed to meet the requirements of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar laws around the world. Region-specific information is in section 12.

## 1. Who we are

The Astro Me mobile app ("Astro Me", "the app", "we", "us") is the controller of your personal data. For any privacy question or request, contact us at ergun.app.dev@gmail.com.

## 2. In short

- When you start using the app, an anonymous account is created for you with no visible sign-up. We do not ask for your email address or phone number.
- To calculate your birth chart, your birth date, time and place are stored on our servers.
- Social features (Soulmate, friends, posting in sign groups) require Sign in with Apple.
- Some readings are generated by AI. Only the information needed for that reading is sent to the AI provider. Your name and account details are never sent.
- Your profile photo and your Moon Journal stay on your phone.
- You can permanently delete your account from inside the app at any time.
- We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account information

- **Anonymous account ID:** On first launch the app receives a randomly generated user ID. Your session uses this ID, and the session key is stored in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain).
- **Abuse prevention:** To stop large numbers of accounts being created from one place, we keep a salted, irreversible hash of your IP address in a short-lived counter. We do not store the IP address itself. We also use Apple's DeviceCheck service to record a single flag (one bit) saying whether your device has already used its free allowances. Apple stores this flag; it does not identify you or your device.
- **Sign in with Apple (optional):** If you sign in with Apple, we store the user ID Apple issues for you, the email address Apple shares on first sign-in (a private relay address if you chose "Hide My Email") and the refresh token Apple provides so that we can revoke your sign-in later. We never see your Apple password.

### 3.2 Profile and birth details

- Your name, used only to greet you in the app and on your Soulmate card. A nickname is fine.
- Your birth date, birth time (or the fact that it is unknown) and birthplace (country, city, district).
- Optional answers you give during onboarding: gender, relationship status and areas of interest.
- Chart positions calculated from your birth details (Sun, Moon, rising sign and other points).

You choose your birthplace from a list. We do not use your device's location services (GPS) and we never collect where you are.

### 3.3 Information created by the features you use

- **Personal readings and reports:** Your personal daily, weekly and monthly readings and your yearly report are stored on our servers so they do not have to be generated again.
- **Luna and Ask the Tarot:** Your question is processed to generate the answer. To track your allowance, we only count how many questions you have asked.
- **Dream Interpretation:** The dream you write, the mood you choose and the interpretation are stored so we can show your dream history and patterns.
- **Palm Reading and Coffee Reading:** The photos you take are sent to our AI provider to generate the reading. **We do not store the photos on our servers.** Only the reading text is stored.
- **Soulmate (only if you join):** Your display name, your age and signs calculated from your birth date, your city, your bio, who you want to see, your likes and passes, your matches, and the people you block or report.
- **Messages:** Messages you write in Soulmate matches, with friends and in sign groups, together with photos, videos and voice messages you send, are stored on our servers so they can be delivered and your chat history can be shown. Reactions and read receipts are stored too.
- **Friends:** Your friend code, friend requests and friend list.
- **Sign groups:** Which groups you have joined and your group preferences.
- **Notifications:** The device notification token Apple provides, so we can send you notifications, and your notification preferences.
- **Premium subscription:** To check whether your subscription is active, we store the transaction ID, product, status and expiry date received from Apple. **We never see your payment details**; Apple handles all payments.

### 3.4 Technical information

Each request to our servers may be logged briefly by our infrastructure provider Cloudflare for security and troubleshooting (for example IP address, time of request and requested address). We do not use these logs to identify you or build a profile.

### 3.5 Information that stays on your phone

These are never sent to our servers: your profile photo, Moon Journal entries, tarot card picks, quiz results, astrology lesson progress, streak counter, app preferences and the local cache of your chats. The small amount of information home screen widgets need is also kept only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes this information.

### 3.6 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your precise or approximate location, health data, advertising identifier (IDFA) or browsing history. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or user tracking tools, so it does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

## 4. Why we use your information and our legal bases

- **To provide the service (performance of a contract):** calculating your chart and readings, keeping your account, delivering your messages and verifying your Premium subscription.
- **Your choice (consent):** joining Soulmate, joining sign groups, receiving notifications and sending photos for Palm or Coffee Reading are all optional. You can withdraw consent at any time, for example by hiding your Soulmate card or turning off notifications.
- **Legitimate interests:** preventing abuse, fake accounts and fraud, preventing misuse of free allowances, reviewing reports and keeping the service secure.
- **Legal obligations:** keeping information or sharing it with authorities when the law requires it.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. AI is used only to write reading texts and to detect prohibited content in messages.

## 5. How we use AI

Luna's answers, personal readings, the yearly report and dream, palm and coffee readings are generated with OpenAI's AI models. What is sent to the AI provider:

- **Luna:** your question, a summary of your chart (planet positions), the day's sky and your onboarding answers about relationships and interests.
- **Ask the Tarot:** your question and the cards you drew.
- **Personal readings and yearly report:** your signs, chart positions and transits.
- **Dream Interpretation:** your dream text, chosen mood and Moon sign.
- **Palm and Coffee Reading:** the photos you send.

**Your name, email address, account ID and exact birth date are not sent to the AI provider.** OpenAI states that it does not use data sent through its API to train its models and keeps it for no more than 30 days for abuse monitoring.

To keep everyone safe, messages and images in chats pass through an automated content check (OpenAI's moderation service). If harassment, hate speech, violence or other prohibited content is detected, the message is removed.

General horoscopes, numerology and compatibility texts are not personal. They are written in advance for everyone and are not linked to you.

## 6. Who can see your information

- **Other users:** If you join Soulmate, other users can see your display name, age, city, signs and bio. Your exact birth date, birth time, birthplace and email are **never** shown. People in a chat can see the messages you write there and your name. You can hide your card at any time with the Show me in Soulmate switch in your profile.
- **Our service providers**, only to run the service and on our instructions:
  - **Cloudflare, Inc.** for server, database and file storage infrastructure.
  - **OpenAI, L.L.C.** for AI reading generation and content moderation.
  - **Apple Inc.** for Sign in with Apple, notification delivery (APNs), DeviceCheck, in-app purchases and subscription verification.
  - **GitHub, Inc.** for hosting these legal pages (no personal data is processed).
- **Legal requirements:** We may share information with competent authorities, only as far as required, when a court order or legal obligation requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information and we do not share it with ad networks or data brokers. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

## 7. International transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, mainly the United States and the European Union. Transfers are protected by our providers' standard contractual clauses and industry-standard security measures. Where your local law requires consent for such transfers, you give it by using the app.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Account, profile, readings, dreams, Soulmate card, friends and messages:** while your account exists or until you delete them. When you unmatch or block someone, your chat with that person and its files are permanently deleted for both of you. You can delete a message for everyone within 1 hour of sending it.
- **Palm and Coffee Reading photos:** not stored by us; processed only while the reading is generated.
- **File upload counters:** 2 days.
- **Account creation counter (IP hash):** short-lived and not linked to you.
- **Reports:** If another user reported you, the report and the messages attached as evidence may be kept even after you delete your account, for as long as needed for safety and legal obligations.

## 9. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from Profile › Delete My Account. Deletion:

- **permanently** deletes your profile, birth details, readings and reports, dream and fortune records, Soulmate card, likes, matches, friendships, the messages and files you sent, your notification token and your subscription record from our servers;
- revokes Sign in with Apple, if you used it;
- clears your chat cache and session on your phone.

Deletion cannot be undone. **Deleting your account does not cancel your Premium subscription.** Cancel it separately on your iPhone in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

## 10. Security

All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data is stored encrypted at rest on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Chat files are not public; they are shown only to members of that chat. Your session key is kept in your phone's secure storage. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is 100% secure, and messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not share sensitive information such as ID numbers, bank details or passwords in chats.

## 11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- find out whether we process your information and request a copy;
- learn why we process it and who we share it with;
- correct inaccurate information (you can edit your profile in the app; birth details can be changed up to 3 times);
- request deletion (you can delete your account in the app right away);
- object to or ask us to restrict processing;
- receive your information in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time;
- challenge an outcome produced by automated analysis and claim compensation if you suffered damage from unlawful processing.

Send requests to ergun.app.dev@gmail.com. To help us verify your identity, we recommend writing from inside the app via Profile › Support, which adds your account ID to the email automatically. We respond within 30 days.

## 12. Region-specific information

- **European Economic Area and United Kingdom:** You have the rights set out in the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
- **California and other US states:** We collect the categories of information described in section 3 for the purposes in section 4. We do not sell or share personal information and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. You have the right to know, delete and correct your information and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent to make a request.
- **Turkey:** This policy also serves as the information notice under KVKK Article 10. You can submit requests under KVKK Article 11 to ergun.app.dev@gmail.com and complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).
- **Brazil, Canada and other countries:** You have the rights granted by your local data protection law (such as LGPD or PIPEDA). Contact us to exercise them.

## 13. Children

Astro Me is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If your country sets a higher minimum age for digital services (up to 16 in some EU countries), you must have a parent's or guardian's permission to use the app if you are below that age. Soulmate is open only to users aged 18 or older, calculated from the birth date. If you learn that a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 14. Notifications

We send notifications only with your permission. You can turn each notification type on or off in Profile › Account and preferences, or turn off all notifications in iPhone Settings. Message notifications may include the sender's name and a short preview and are delivered through Apple's notification service. Daily horoscope reminders are scheduled on your phone without using our servers.

## 15. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available in the app and on this page, with the "Last updated" date at the top. We will announce important changes in the app.
