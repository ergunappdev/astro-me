# Soulastra: delete your account and data

_Last updated: October 21, 2026_

This page explains how to delete your **Soulastra** account (developer: ergun.app.dev), or only some of your data, and what happens to it.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open **Soulastra**.
2. Go to **Profile › Delete My Account**.
3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

## Delete your account without the app

If you uninstalled the app or can't open it, email **[ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20account)** with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Include anything that helps us find your account: your account ID (shown in the support email the app prepares under Profile › Support), your friend code, your Soulmate display name, or the email address of your Google or Apple sign-in. We delete the account within **30 days** and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account **permanently** removes from our servers:

- your profile and birth details (date, time, place) and your calculated chart;
- your readings and reports, dream records and coffee, palm and tarot readings;
- your Soulmate card and photo, likes and matches;
- your friendships, the messages and files you sent, and your chat settings;
- your notification token and notification preferences;
- your subscription record;
- the link to your Google or Apple sign-in.

On your phone, the app clears your session and chat cache.

## What is kept, and for how long

- **Reports by other users:** if another user reported you, the report and the messages attached as evidence may be kept for as long as needed for safety and legal obligations.
- **Short-lived security counters** (a salted, irreversible hash of your IP address and file upload counters): deleted automatically within 2 days; they are not linked to you.
- **Purchases:** Google Play and Apple keep their own purchase records under their policies. **Deleting your account does not cancel your Premium subscription.** Cancel it on Android in the Google Play Store app › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or on iPhone in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

## Delete only some of your data

You can delete parts of your data without deleting your account:

- **Readings:** in your reading history, or on a dream reading, tap Delete.
- **Soulmate photo:** remove or replace your photo in Soulmate; it is deleted from our servers. Leaving Soulmate hides your card from everyone; to have the card itself deleted, email us (see below).
- **Chats:** unmatching or blocking someone permanently deletes your chat and its files for both of you; you can delete a message for everyone within 1 hour of sending it.
- **Anything else:** email [ergun.app.dev@gmail.com](mailto:ergun.app.dev@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20data) with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us what to delete. We do it within 30 days.
